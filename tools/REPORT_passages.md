## Manual QA (2026-09-24; polished after two QA reviews, last 2026-09-25)

- **Source and build.** The 60 passages are hand-written for this pack (20 per
  level). The authoring sources in `.cache/src/` (gitignored) assemble into
  `tools/passages_src.json`; the builder writes `pack/passages.json` and this
  report. Two runs give byte-identical output. Orthography follows the pack's
  own sentences: ZWNJ in می‌روم, کتاب‌ها, خانه‌ی (ezafe after silent ه),
  ثبت‌نام, کم‌کم, هیچ‌کس, هیچ‌وقت, Persian yeh/kaf, tanwin only where the pack word has it
  (لطفاً, معمولاً), standard written forms (not Tehrani colloquial).
- **Word counts.** `words` is the builder's count (the band rule); `ws_words`
  is the whitespace count the app shows. All 60 passages are in band by both
  (A1 ws 61-84, A2 92-106, B1 116-141). The tightest (p0008 63, p0016 63,
  p0013 64, p0033 92) carry a few words of margin.
- **Builder, core.** Spans own the combining marks (Unicode Mn/Mc) that follow
  them and never start on one (`passages._mark_bounds`), so the tanwin of
  لطفاً, حتماً, قبلاً, معمولاً, تقریباً, اصلاً sits inside its span (0 spans are
  followed by a mark). Latin and Cyrillic text in NFC has no marks, so their
  spans are unchanged. The report gained the `ws_words` column.
- **Builder, Persian.** Passage-only hooks in `langs/fa.py` (`passage_retag`,
  `passage_post_resolve`, `passage_lemma_alias`); the corpus build never
  calls them. What they fix, each seen in these passages:
  - an X-tagged plain word (فردا شب, به سر کار) gets its dictionary class;
  - a verb read with a non-infinitive lemma, or a past-tense verb read as
    noun + copula (چه خواست؟), is re-read from its surface;
  - an ordinal the pack lacks (سیزدهم) is a numeral;
  - بهتر/بیشتر/کمتر (and -ین) keep their own lemma, not به "to";
  - a light-verb compound the pack lacks splits into its parts (دوست شد);
  - a noun that is a bare preposition's object does not form a finite
    compound (بعد از غذا بخورید is خوردن, not غذا خوردن);
  - a non-pack reading that is a pack word plus indefinite/ezafe ی
    (زن آرامی, کودکی‌اش, کارت دانشجویی), a comparative (مهم‌تری, بزرگ‌ترها)
    or a preposition plus clitic (برایت) links that pack word;
  - a pack noun X+ی the tagger kept whole (ماهی, گوشی) stays that noun only
    when the passage English names its gloss ("a large fish", "a new phone").
    The flag is set in fix_sentence on passage rows only. Otherwise it is X +
    indefinite ی (یک دوستی "a friend", ماهی یک بار "once a month").
    Adjective stems (روز خوبی: خوب) and participles (داده, نوشته, بسته) keep
    their stem;
  - نه is "nine" after ساعت, before a bare unit noun (نه سال, نه نفر) or after
    از/تا with a number in the sentence (از نه تا پنج); elsewhere it is "not"
    (a unit with ی is no count: نه هفته‌ای یک بار);
  - در before آن/این + را is the noun "door, lid" (در آن را ببندید);
  - an infinitive with a pronoun clitic is a verb, so its compound forms
    (درست کردنش: درست کردن);
  - a noun compound of two pack nouns the pack lacks as a whole links its head
    (ثبت‌نام: ثبت);
  - ساله is سال; پزیدن (Wiktionary's infinitive on the stem پز) is پختن.
- **Names.** Persian has no capitals, so the core's capitalised-name rule
  never fires. Every declared name is also an `oop` entry with the reason
  "name"; the builder then skips it (not counted, not linked, not reported).
  No name is spelled like a pack word.
- **Questions.** 286 questions (mc 143, tf 143). Every question's `words` are
  linked in its `sentence`. No true/false item shares a 5-word run with any
  passage sentence. No correct option appears in its passage title. Questions
  about a first-person narrator name them نویسنده. No item is answerable
  from world knowledge alone (the Nowruz items ask what the passage says).
  Correct mc options that repeat 4+ words of a sentence: A1 2/42, A2 6/41,
  B1 11/60. The correct option is the unique longest in A1 6/42, A2 7/41,
  B1 12/60 (17% overall).
- **Balance.** Correct mc answers sit at positions 0/1/2/3 as 36/36/36/35.
  True/false answers are 67 true and 76 false (A1 22/27, A2 25/29, B1 20/20).
- **Out-of-pack lemmas.** فسنجان x1 in p0053 (the dish's name). Everything
  else counted is a pack word.
- **Authoring conventions (tagger limits, avoided in the text).** The corpus
  rule "no compound after a numeral" also blocks ساعت پنج کار می‌کنم and سه
  بر دو برنده شدیم, so the passages keep numerals away from light verbs. The
  present stem گرد is decided by the English; 2pl بگردید/می‌گردید contain
  گردید and skip that rule, so they are not used. ADJ+ی homographs of pack
  nouns (خوبی, بدی) resolve to the adjective. خواهم + stem future is avoided
  (it links خواستن). "به خوبی", "سر و صدا", "رو به" are avoided. "کمی" after
  a noun (few) reads as the adverb "a little", so "مشتری زیادی نداشت" is used.
